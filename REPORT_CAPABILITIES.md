# Current course-mode routes (Gazette v3)

- register: Settings-selected current-term reference PDF gazette, with cumulative GP/CH/CGPA from complete imported history and fee appendix at the end.
- cgpa_list: roll-number-wise all-semester GPA/CGPA, name, program, optional source semester number, credits and subject names; PDF / Word / Excel.
- term, cohort, degree, department, subjects, repeats, summer, validation, mapping: source registers and audits. Department register requires a supplied department field.

Legacy report categories below remain separate; their official standing/ranking decisions are not inferred from course attempts.
